# Project design brief

## Product
Obsidian Studio

## Type
Brand-forward product or studio site

## Audience
Prospective clients, collaborators, or users evaluating quality and taste

## Goal
Create a distinct, premium impression and drive interest or contact

## First viewport
- Strong brand presence
- One dominant visual anchor
- One sharp headline
- One short supporting sentence
- One clear CTA group

## Narrative sequence
- Brand impression
- What it is
- Why it matters
- Proof or work
- Final CTA

## Visual direction
- Dark, premium, cinematic restraint
- Strong contrast
- Memorable composition
- Minimal but intentional motion
- Distinctive without becoming noisy

## Avoid
- Generic agency-site layouts
- Floating card clutter
- Overexplaining in the hero
- Too many equal-priority sections
- Empty style without clear purpose
